The prop spawner lets you spawn any sort of props, anywhere you want. Make yourself a fortress. Please avoid using the prop spawner when non-admin/friends (publics) are playing, they are here to play the game, not build-a-fort. 

## Getting Started

1. Enter **/admin** in chat to open the admin menu
2. Click on **Spawn Props** _(not the Spawn Objects menu)_

## Spawning a prop

When you select a prop to spawn, it will show you a pink ghost preview - the prop will be placed exactly where you look when you press :u-kbd[Shift + Use].

There are multiple ways to select an object. You can see the most recently globally used prop, search for one by name, or view them by category.

Some categories have a secondary category inside (Bodies)

1. Press **Spawn Props**
2. Press **Prop List**
3. Select the category you want
4. Select the item in the category to spawn
5. Position your mouse where you want it to spawn
6. Rotate the prop how you want.
7. Once satisfied, spawn it with :u-kbd[Use]

<h2 id="keybinds" class="my-1">Keybind Cheat Sheet</h2>

### Global
- :u-kbd[ZOOM(MMM) (Scope in)] - Change mode (Move/Rotate, Color, Freelook)
- :u-kbd[Use(E)] - Place
- :u-kbd[Walk(Shift) + Use(E)] - Cancel placement
- :u-kbd[Duck(Ctrl) + Use(E)] - Change placement mode (physics, dynamic)
### Move / Rotate
- :u-kbd[Attack(LMM)] - Push away from you
- :u-kbd[Shove(RMM)] - Bring closer to you
- :u-kbd[Reload(R)] - Hold to rotate object, moving horizontally OR vertically
- :u-kbd[Reload(R) + Attack(LMM)] - Cycle axis of rotation
- :u-kbd[Reload(R) + Shove(RMM)] - Cycle snapping angle
- :u-kbd[Walk(Shift) + Reload(R)] - Toggle collision on/off
- :u-kbd[Duck(Ctrl) + Reload(R)] - Toggle auto-rotate on/off
- :u-kbd[Jump(SPACE) + Reload(R)] - Cycle stacker modes
