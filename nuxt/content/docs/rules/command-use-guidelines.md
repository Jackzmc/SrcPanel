The following guidelines are intended for using commands in **official campaign maps**. In custom maps, its less of an issue.

These are guidelines, not rules, as they are not intended to never allow you to do these things. There are always exceptions, these are just meant to have everyone on the same page of what is okay and what is not. 

**The general principles of the guidelines**

1.  Use cheats in moderation, do not abuse it, or rely on them.
2.  If it affects others or the team, it's a problem.
3.  If the team as a whole is okay with it, then it's fine. Don't do actions for what _you_ want, without considering the team. (No pubs)
4.  Finales and customs are okay to goof off a little more, but don't be excessive.
5.  As a group we try not to take too much away from the game itself. Keep it fun for everyone.
6.  When pubs are playing, they should not be effected by any admin powers while playing. _Exceptions: /sorry responses._ If there's a troll, use /fta appropriately or discuss it with the rest of the team first.
7.  Do not abuse others for being new players. (Everyone was new at some point).

### Guidelines

* **No slaying specials**
    * Kicking specials is the same as slaying them.
    * If you're far ahead and got pinned, own it, it happens. (The game is called Left 4 Dead for a reason.)
* **No slaying tanks**  
    * In custom maps, sometimes they spawn horribly early you can slay
    * In some finales, they spawn way too close to escape vehicle you can slay

* **No killing yourself and respawning just to restore health**
* **No reviving yourself, especially when others are coming to help, and it's not intentional.**
* **No noclipping around, it's distracting.**
* **No charging players excessively or at inappropriate times.**
* **Hats are OK, but don't pick up large objects that block people's views; it's annoying. Don't over-use hats.**
* **Spawning props, especially large physics ones that interfere with people (in the way, hurts them, etc.) is NOT ok.**
* **Do not give yourself big guns, ammo, or throwables during any normal play through.**
* **Teleporting players, incapped or not**  
    * If they aren't incapped, teleporting players forcefully (excluding exceptions), is not OK
    * Some exceptions: Ladder bugs, glitches, saferoom warps, or they ask for it
    



* **Respawning dead players**
    * If you or someone else dies during a normal game or simply by the games mechanics, respawning other players or yourself is NOT okay. 
    * Some exceptions: ladder bugs, game glitches, or playing in custom maps.
