---
title: Adding Players to the Chat Group
type: procedure
---
If you think someone should be invited to the **chat** group and you believe most people seem to be on the same page then you may start a discussion where everyone can vote on them being added.

If anyone has something to say, they can leave a comment. Recommended to keep discussion on **Single Choice**, add "Yes" and "No" options, and keep **Respondants* hidden.

If you add their steamid2 (STEAM_#:#:######) to description, it will show a user tooltip for them

[Click here to start a discussion](/discuss/new?title=Add+a+Player&comments=hide&respondants=hide&responses=hide)

## Critera for adding a player

* They must have already played minimum of 25 hours on the server
* If they were previously discussed on, it has been a month since then the vote ended
* The vote must last minimum 2 weeks
