---
title: Adding Players to the Steam Group
type: procedure
---
If you think someone should be invited to the **steam** group, as you think they have potential you may add them to the steam group at your discretion. The steam group allows us to get used to them and see if they fit.

To add them, send them the group link https://steamcommunity.com/groups/jackzl4d2 and tell them to request to join. Then someone with access must accept their invite

We can also grant them some limited admin powers (chat, kick, and seeing admin activity) if we think they are a good player and have played with us a few times.
